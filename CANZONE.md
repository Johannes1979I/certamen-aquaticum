## PROMPT MUSICALE PER SUNO

### Campo «Style of Music» (versione corta)

```
Italian summer pop hit 2026, upbeat reggaeton-pop, 102 BPM, tropical house
plucks, bright brass stabs, handclaps, whistle hook, male and female duet,
huge anthemic singalong chorus, children's choir on final chorus, poolside
festival energy, sunny polished production
```

### Versione lunga (se il campo lo permette)

```
Italian summer smash hit, 2026 radio pop with reggaeton and tropical house
influences. 102 BPM, key of A minor turning bright on the chorus. Verses:
half-time dembow groove, muted guitar, marimba and finger snaps, playful.
Pre-chorus: rising synth riser, drums drop out, handclap build. Chorus:
full four-on-the-floor, wide brass stabs, tropical plucked lead, whistle
counter-melody, layered gang vocals. Male lead with a warm raspy tone,
female lead answering on the chorus, kids choir doubling the last chorus.
Bridge: strip to plucked guitar and voice, then explode back in. Bright,
sunny, celebratory, made for singing along around a swimming pool.
```

### Impostazioni consigliate

| Campo | Cosa mettere |
|---|---|
| Title | `Certamen Aquaticum (Estate all'Holiday)` |
| Lyrics | il testo qui sopra, tag `[...]` compresi |
| Exclude styles | `trap, drill, sad, ballad, lo-fi, autotune heavy, metal` |
| Instrumental | no |

### Consigli pratici

- **Le lingue**: scrivi nel prompt *«sung in Italian»* se le prime prove
  escono con accento straniero.
- **La sirena** del pre-ritornello: se vuoi che si senta davvero, aggiungi
  `whistle blow, air horn` allo stile.
- Genera **due o tre versioni** e tieni quella col ritornello più orecchiabile:
  è quello che i ragazzi canteranno a bordo vasca.
- Per la **sigla d'apertura** basta un taglio di 40-50 secondi: intro +
  primo ritornello. Suno esporta il brano intero, poi lo accorci.
